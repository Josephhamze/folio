# Privacy

Folio collects nothing about you, on the Mac, iPhone or iPad.

- **Your files stay where you keep them.** Folio opens and saves PDFs in place, in the folders, iCloud Drive or other locations you choose, and sends them nowhere. A file leaves your device only when you share it, and only to where you send it.
- **Your signatures stay on your device.** Your signature and initials, and the page each file was left on, are kept in Folio's own settings on your device.
- **No network.** Folio makes no network connections of its own: no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Questions go to [Folio's issues on GitHub](https://github.com/Josephhamze/folio/issues).
