# Folio for iPhone and iPad: design

## Intent

The owner wants Folio on the App Store, for everyone, on iPhone and iPad, beside the Mac app. It is the same app: the same documents, marks, matched text and signatures, used with a finger or Apple Pencil instead of a mouse. The Mac app must not change in behaviour.

Mainland China is left out at launch: its App Store needs an ICP filing, which needs a Chinese company. It is one setting in App Store Connect, so it can be added after a filing.

## Goals

**One code base**
- The same Swift files build both apps. The Mac app builds as now (`swift build`, `build.sh`, the dmg). The iOS app builds from an Xcode project generated by XcodeGen from `project.yml`.
- Platform differences sit behind `#if os(macOS)` and `#if os(iOS)`, in as few places as possible. Markup, matched text, signatures, page operations and undo stay in one place, used by both.
- iOS 26 and later. iPhone and iPad, every orientation, and iPad multitasking.

**Opening and saving**
- Folio opens on the system's document browser (Recents, Browse), with Folio's name and colours on it. PDFs open from Files, in place, and from other apps' share sheets (Open in Folio).
- Create Document makes a blank page, A4, or Letter where the region measures in US units.
- Changes save themselves. Rename, Duplicate, Move and Export come from the system's title menu over the document.
- Each file reopens at the page it was left on.

**Reading**
- Pages scroll continuously and pinch to zoom.
- The toolbar shows the page box ("3 of 12"; type a number to go there) and a search field: Find counts the matches and steps through them.
- Thumbnails, bookmarks and comments live in an inspector: a column at the side on iPad, a sheet on iPhone.
- A protected file asks for its password over the pages, as on the Mac.

**Marking up**
- A bar at the bottom of the pages holds the same 15 tools and the colour as the Mac, scrolling sideways where it doesn't fit, with Undo, Redo, and Delete while a mark is selected.
- With a drawing tool on, one finger or the Pencil draws, and two fingers scroll and zoom.
  - Highlight, Underline and Strike Through: drag across the text.
  - Rectangle, Oval, Line, Arrow and Redact: drag out the shape.
  - Pen: draw.
  - Sign and Initial: tap to place at the usual size, or drag a box to size it. With none saved yet, the pad opens first: draw it with a finger or the Pencil, or type it.
  - Note: tap. The comments open with the note's field ready to type.
  - Text Box: tap and type. The words take the font, size and colour of the nearest text, and sit on its line, by the Mac's rules. Return, or a tap elsewhere, ends the typing. Leaving the document or the app keeps the words.
  - Edit Text: tap a line to type over it.
- With the Select tool, a tap selects a mark and outlines it. Drag it to move it, Delete deletes it, the colour well recolours it, and a double tap on a text box edits its words. Form fields fill with PDFKit's own controls, each fill one undo step.
- Every change can be undone: the bar's buttons, the system's undo gestures, and iPad's Edit menu.

**Sharing and printing**
- A share button hands the PDF as it is now, edits included, to the share sheet: AirDrop, Mail, Save to Files, Print.
- On iPad's menu bar, Folio's menus work as on the Mac where they apply: Find, the View menu's zoom and page layouts, Tools › Edit Signature and Edit Initials, and File › Print (⌘P).

**App Store**
- The app icon is a full-bleed 1024 px square drawn by `icon.swift`: the Mac icon's art without its squircle and shadow, which iOS adds itself. No transparency.
- Info.plist: a PDF document type (Editor, Alternate rank), opening in place, the document browser, a launch screen, and no non-exempt encryption (PDFKit's encryption is Apple's own).
- A privacy manifest: no tracking and no data collected. It declares UserDefaults, which keeps signatures and last pages, under reason CA92.1.
- Bundle ID `com.josephhamze.folio`, as on the Mac. Team PP6TZWTTLG. Version 1.0, build 1.
- `./build.sh ios` builds Folio and runs it in the iOS simulator. `./build.sh ios-release` archives it and uploads it to App Store Connect.
- App Store screenshots for the 6.9-inch iPhone and 13-inch iPad, a privacy policy page (`PRIVACY.md`), and the listing's text.

## Non-goals

- Keyboard tool keys, arrow nudging and Esc on iPad. The menu bar's commands do work.
- Changing a text box's font or size on iOS (the Mac's Show Fonts).
- Read Mode, Save As and the home window on iOS. The title menu's Duplicate stands in for Save As, and the document browser for the home window.
- Organizing pages, and the passwords and properties sheets: they are on the roadmap for both apps.
- Folio for iPad running on Macs: there is a real Mac app. Its availability is turned off in App Store Connect.
- The Mac App Store, Mac Catalyst, localisation and mainland China.

## Anti-goals

- A Mac app that behaves differently, or a build, test or dmg that stops working.
- Lost work on iOS: words being typed, or edits, when the app goes to the background or the document closes.
- Two copies of any logic that isn't about input or layout.
- New frameworks or packages. SwiftUI, UIKit, PDFKit only. XcodeGen is a build tool, already installed.

## Decided by the builder

- **Project.** `project.yml` describes one iOS app target and one iOS test target. The app takes `Sources/Folio` as a synced folder, so new files need no project edit. `Folio.xcodeproj` is generated and gitignored. iOS-only files are `iOS/Info.plist`, `iOS/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` and `iOS/Assets.xcassets`.
- **Names.** On iOS, `Platform.swift` names UIKit's colour, font, image and Bézier path types with the AppKit names the shared code uses (`NSColor` is `UIColor`, and so on), so shared code reads as it does today. Where the two APIs differ, the call site has its own `#if`.
- **Mac-only code.** The home window, the Fonts panel, Save As, Read Mode, Finder and window code, and the mouse and key handling are compiled for macOS only.
- **The canvas.** `Canvas` stays one class, a `PDFView`. What a tool does at a point on a page (begin, extend, end, place) moves out of the mouse handlers into shared methods. The Mac's mouse handlers and the iOS gestures both call them. iOS input lives in its own file: one pan and one tap gesture, which take precedence over PDFKit's scrolling only when the tool needs them. For markup, the drag's text comes from `PDFPage.selection(from:to:)`. iOS edits text in a `UITextField` over the box, ended by Return, a tap elsewhere, a change of scene phase or the document closing.
- **Layout.** On iOS, `DocumentView` puts the page view under a navigation bar (page box, search, the inspector's button, share) and the tool bar at the bottom. The panes go in `.inspector`, and the home window becomes a `DocumentGroupLaunchScene`.
- **Last pages.** On iOS they are kept for files that still exist, since iOS has no recent-documents list.
- **Tests.** The Mac tests stay as they are and cover the shared logic. A small iOS test target covers what touch adds: drag to highlight, drag to draw a shape as one undo step, tap to add a note, a text box that joins only with words, and drag to move a selected mark.
